Work in progress, but:

[Engine Hum](/engine-hum/)

<!--more-->

It has a few adjustable parameters, but mostly it's a self-contained webpage
that can generate what is hopefullly a soothing take on a sci-fi engine room,
with optional visual effects. To dismiss the dialogue box in the middle of the
screen, tap or click outside the box. To bring it back, tap or click anywhere.

This is just a fun project I did in my spare time, inspired by the months of not
feeling like doing much more than recovering in the recliner, and looking for
quiet, soothing sounds to take my mind off of being uncomfortable after some
surgery. I was watching some old reruns, heard a high-tech engine room sound and
thought, y'know, that kinda sounds like slow breathing...I bet that'd be a great
place to take a nap.

And here we are.

Depending on your platform, you may be able to install this from Chrome.
